Astra

Privacy Policy

Astra collects nothing and sends nothing. There is no account to create, no server to talk to, and no networking code in the app at all. Everything you record stays on your device.

What Astra stores

The habits you name, the days you marked them kept, and the stars those days lit. Along with that, a few settings: whether you've seen the opening, the reminder time you chose, and the order your constellations arrive in, which is fixed the first time you open the app.

All of it is written to a database inside the app's own storage on your device, in a container shared with the Astra widget so the widget can show the same day you're looking at. Nothing is written anywhere else.

What Astra does not do

It doesn't collect personal information. It doesn't track you, across this app or any other. It contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs of any kind. It makes no network requests, so there is nothing it could transmit even by accident. The star catalogue it draws from ships inside the app, which is why the sky works with no connection at all.

Location

Astra never asks for your location and never reads it. The sky it builds is anchored to a fixed point chosen once, on the device, and that anchor never leaves the device either.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications, scheduled by the app on your device and delivered by iOS. They are not push notifications, no server is involved, and nothing about them is reported back.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, Apple's backup includes Astra's data along with every other app's. That backup is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not this one. Astra itself has no iCloud sync and does not upload your data.

Deleting your data

Delete the app and everything it stored is removed with it. Because no copy was ever sent anywhere, there is nothing else to request, export, or ask anyone to erase.

Children

Astra is safe for any age. It collects no data from anyone, including children under 13, because it collects no data from anyone at all.

Changes

If a future version of Astra ever collects anything, this page will say so before that version ships, and the date below will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to the issue tracker.
