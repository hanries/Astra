# Reply to the 4.3(a) rejection

Submission cd04b860, build 1.0 (2), reviewed 19 August 2026.

Apple's finding: the app "shares a similar binary, metadata, and/or concept as
apps previously submitted by a terminated Apple Developer Program account".

Confirmed with the developer before writing this: one Apple Developer account
has ever existed in his name, this one; the earlier app Mochi was submitted on
this same account; and no device, network, payment method, phone number or
address is shared with any other developer account. On that basis the finding
appears to be a mistaken association, and the reply below says so plainly and
offers evidence rather than argument.

Check the two bracketed details against App Store Connect before sending.

---

Thank you for the review. I would like to respond to the 4.3(a) finding, which
I believe is a mistaken association.

This is the only Apple Developer Program account I have ever held. I have never
submitted an app under any other account, my own or anyone else's, and I have
previously shipped [Mochi] from this same account. The Mac I build on, the
network I submit from, and the payment and contact details on the account are
mine and are not shared with any other developer.

Astra was written by me from an empty repository. The full development history
is public at https://github.com/hanries/Astra, starting with the first commit on
31 July 2026 and running to the submitted build across 30 commits under a single
author. The history shows the app being built from nothing, including the
revisions and mistakes along the way, which I hope is useful evidence of
original authorship.

The app contains no third-party code. There are no Swift Package dependencies,
no CocoaPods, no Carthage, and no third-party SDKs of any kind. This is
verifiable in the Xcode project file in that repository.

The app also contains no purchased or third-party assets, and is not built on a
template. Every image in it is produced by a script that is itself in the
repository: Tools/build_catalog.py builds the star data from the public Yale
Bright Star Catalogue, Tools/make_icon.py renders the app icon, and
Tools/make_launch_mark.swift renders the launch screen artwork. The star
portraits are not image files at all. They are drawn procedurally at run time
from each star's catalogued values.

If Astra is being associated with a specific app or account, I would be grateful
if you could tell me which one, so that I can respond to it directly. I am happy
to provide anything further that would help, including a walkthrough of the
source history or a build from a clean checkout of that repository.

Thank you for your time.

---

## Before sending

- Confirm Mochi's name and status in App Store Connect. If it was never
  released, say "an earlier app of mine on this same account" instead of naming
  it, or drop the clause.
- Keep github.com/hanries/Astra public until this is resolved, and do not
  force-push or rewrite history. The commit history is the evidence.

## In parallel

App Review usually cannot see the account-association data that triggered a
4.3(a). Contact Apple Developer Program Support separately at
developer.apple.com/contact and ask them to look at the account association
directly.

## If the reply fails

Appeal to the App Review Board from the same submission page. Send the same
facts, unchanged. Do not open a second developer account: that is the pattern
the guideline describes and it would put both accounts at risk.
